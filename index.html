How Does Rehla Work?

A seamless experience designed for both travelers and service providers.

1

Search & Discover

Browse hundreds of verified trips. Filter by destination, budget, date, trip type (Safari, Nile Cruise, Day-use) and ratings.

2

Book & Pay Securely

Choose your trip and complete checkout with Credit/Debit Cards, E-wallets, or Fawry — all through our secure payment gateway.

3

Track & Enjoy

Track your booking status in real time, receive push notifications, then explore Egypt and leave a verified review after your trip.

1

Register & List Trips

Submit your legal documents, get approved, then use our dashboard to list trips, set pricing, upload photos, and define schedules.

2

Receive Bookings

Booking requests arrive automatically. Review customer details, confirm or update reservation status, and communicate with travelers.

3

Earn & Withdraw

Your earnings are tracked in your financial wallet after each successful booking. Request payouts directly to your bank account anytime.
