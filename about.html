Who We Are

The Rehla Story

A comprehensive travel platform built to bridge the gap between curious travelers and trusted local experts.

Rehla — Arabic for "journey" — was born from a simple but powerful observation: domestic tourism in Egypt is thriving, yet the experience of finding and booking a trustworthy trip remains frustrating and fragmented. Travelers scroll endlessly through social media, contact agencies manually, and have no reliable way to compare prices or read verified reviews. Agencies, meanwhile, struggle to reach wider audiences and manage bookings without burning out on manual operations.

We built Rehla to fix that. The platform consists of two core experiences: a beautiful, intuitive app for travelers to discover destinations, filter trips by their preferences, book securely, and track their journey — and a powerful vendor dashboard for agencies to list services, receive automated bookings, manage their earnings, and grow with actionable analytics.

Through advanced tools, real-time communication, and a smooth user experience, Rehla empowers travelers to find verified trips instantly, while giving service providers the digital infrastructure they've always needed. Whether you're exploring or offering — Rehla brings you closer.

Why We Exist

The Problem We Solve

😓 The Challenges

  • ✕ Scattered reviews across social media with no verification — travelers can't trust what they read.
  • ✕ No unified platform to compare prices for domestic Egyptian trips.
  • ✕ No secure online payment system for local travel bookings.
  • ✕ Agencies struggle to reach wider audiences beyond their existing social following.
  • ✕ Manual booking management is time-consuming and error-prone for travel businesses.

✨ The Rehla Solution

  • ✓ Verified, post-trip reviews that travelers can trust — no fake ratings.
  • ✓ One searchable marketplace with transparent pricing and smart filters.
  • ✓ Secure checkout with Cards, E-Wallets, and Fawry integration.
  • ✓ A dedicated vendor dashboard giving agencies a professional digital presence.
  • ✓ Automated booking management, real-time notifications, and payout wallets.
